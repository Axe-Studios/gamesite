###  What is Encounters?
Encounters is the working name for the game project I am working on.  It is set in roughly modern times, and follows the stories of people living in a small town, tentatively named Maplefield.  The player character is a teen who spends the game learning about God, themselves, faith, humanity and what it means to live as a Christian.

The town of Maplefield is more than just the setting for the game.  It is intended to feel like a real community, full of heart, and filled with ordinary people living ordinary lives.  It is the kind of town where everyone knows each other, and there are few secrets on the surface.  However, people are always more than the "self" they show in public.  Due to some extraordinary circumstances the player character will have the opportunity to get to know the citizens in deep and personal ways.

There is no grand quest to save the world, just the opportunity to share the lives of the townspeople and experience unique ways in which God can answer our prayers.

Game play consists of 2 major aspects:
1) Slice of life - The player character moves around town, interacts with friends, family, locals, getting to know the people, engaging in everyday activities and just living life.
2) The "fantasy" elements.  While average in almost every way, the player character has a unique aspect to them that translates into their life being anything but ordinary.  I won't get into this too deeply to avoid spoilers and the like, but this is where more RPG like elements come into play.  There is some combat (no gore or killing), adventure, puzzles and mysteries to solve.

I'll share more about the setting and narrative in the future.

### Who are you?
Well, I'm Joe.  I'm a tinkerer in many arenas, but by trade, I am a software developer.  I've been building business software since the late 90s, working with everything from FoxPro to VB to C++ to PHP to Java to C#.

I don't have a formal education, meaning I am predominantly self-taught.  That is not to say I learned what I know on my own.  There have been a whole host of mentors, co-workers, friends and family that have helped and guided me on this journey.

I have also tinkered with game development since I started programming, but never sought to do it as a profession.  I started with Pascal and text-based games a few decades ago.  Then came C++ and OpenGL 1.1.  Later came UDK, then XNA, then Unity and Unreal.  I built a lot of prototypes and experiments over the years, but never really tried to build a complete game.  For me, the fun was in trying to build specific systems.  However, if all of my prototypes were put together, it'd be pretty close to containing everything needed for an RPG like game.

### Why am I creating this?
In early 2026, I was laid off from my job of 11 years.  It was a really good job for a long time, but the last 2 years were rough.  I was promoted into a position that I never wanted and worked with a couple of rough clients.  My health was declining, and I honestly believe the job was a huge contributor to that.  However, I was not brave enough to leave the stability it offered.  So, I prayed that God would help me get out of that situation, and did nothing to actually make that happen myself.  Selfish huh? :)

Well God answered my prayer, and I do genuinely see it that way.  I was unemployed for the first time in a long time.  However, with severance and savings I was in a position to not be in a hurry to find something new.  So, I spent a month or 2 focusing on my health and praying for God to guide me in what comes next.  While looking for a job and dealing with health issues, I spent a lot of my free time getting back into game development.

The software job market in 2026 is.. less than awesome.  Jobs are scarce, layoffs are in the news often, so finding a new job is harder than I've ever seen it.  Frankly, I don't want to go back either.  The industry is moving in directions I am not a fan of, and I'm just burned out.

So, I prayed and I tinkered and I consulted with people I trust.  Encounters is the outcome of that.  Unlike before, I believe that I have been genuinely seeking God's will and his plan for my life.

I won't get into the details here, but creating this game is what I believe to be my current calling.  For now, I'm working on it alone, with the goal to produce a prototype and vertical slice by year end or shortly after.  That is an ambitious goal, and not one that I can make succeed on my own.  However, I have faith that God will open the necessary doors and continue to give me guidance to keep the project moving forward.  For now, I'm tightening my belt, living frugally and coding like mad.

When the prototype is complete, I hope to pitch it to various Christian organizations, with the hope that one or more will be willing to help fund it with a modest budget.  I may also look to something like Patreon eventually, but would rather not do things that way for now.

I'm not looking to launch a second career with this game, nor do I plan to make a lot of money with it.  I'd be more than happy just to see it released.  Ultimately, I don't even want to retain ownership.  I'm just the steward for the time being, and I want any credit to go to God.  I want people to experience the game and hopefully learn about God's love, what it means to be a Christian and why people are worth loving.  Ideally, the game would be given away for free, but that likely won't be my decision.

I realize that from a practical perspective this project doesn't have a high chance of success.  However, God can open doors, and unlike before, I am completely putting myself and the game in God's hands.  I am also not sitting idle.  I will do everything that I can to see it through.

### What is this site for?
This site is here to serve a few purposes.
1) It serves as a development blog.  I hope to generate regular automated updates that summarize the commit messages (code and project changes) that I make to the prototype.  I also hope to post more blog entries like this.  These will more directly showcase progress, including screen shots, videos, game play system descriptions, technical breakdowns and the like.
2) It serves as a time line.  Whenever I do start seeking funding, this site will serve as evidence of my activities starting in 08/2026.  The hope is that by showing frequent updates and regular progress, people will understand the project is not some pie in the sky idea, but a project that I am investing months of my time and personal finances into.

### Where does the project stand at the moment?
The prototype already contains much of the underlying framework, including character systems, combat, input, animation, inventories, data-driven definitions, world state, logging, and developer tooling. Future posts will dive into these systems in more detail.

Work to date has looked like the following:
- May
  - Get up to speed on what has changed in Unity in the last few years
  - Take stock of existing projects and their capabilities
  - Get several projects running on modern Unity versions
- June
  - Start migrating some core systems to a modern Unity version and subsystems
  - Start blending migrated systems from different projects together into a more cohesive whole
- July
  - Get my house in order.  This consisted of finishing the migration, modernization and consolidation of existing components into the foundation for a full project
  - Start figuring out what Encounters wants to be, both from a narrative and gameplay perspective
- August
  - Start on Encounters in earnest

If you check the Development Logs section, you can see the commit summaries from July onward.

As mentioned earlier, I am a programmer by trade.  While I am familiar with art and sound asset creation, that is not my skill set.  For the time being, and likely for the duration of the prototype, most in game assets will be pulled from purchased asset packs.

Well, that about does it for now.  My hope is this site will accurately and honestly document the journey to create Encounters.

Thank you for taking the time to read this, and I hope you will continue to follow Encounters as development progresses.
